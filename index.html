🐢 Turtle Tides

gather coconuts & shells, mind the birds, bank it all at home

How to Play

  • 🐢 Roam the island freely and walk over coconuts and shells to pick them up.
  • 🏖️ The beach near home is safe. 🌳 The woods are riskier — birds patrol there.
  • 🎒 You can only carry so much at once. Watch your carry meter.
  • 🏠 Walk back onto your rock-pile home to bank everything you're carrying — banked items are safe forever.
  • 🦅 If a bird touches you in the woods, you respawn at home and lose only that trip's unbanked haul.
  • 🥥 Coconuts refill your hunger automatically when it runs low. No coconuts? You just move a little slower.
  • 🐚 Spend banked shells at home to upgrade your home, carry capacity, and shell color.
