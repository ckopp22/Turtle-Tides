Need help or want to report a bug? Email caden.kopp@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version.

Common questions

How do I move? Drag anywhere on the screen to use the floating joystick.

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the app erases your saves.

There's no sound. Check that your phone isn't on silent, and that sound and music are turned on with the buttons in the game.
