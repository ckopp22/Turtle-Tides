Turtle Tides Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Turtle Tides is a single-player game. We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and sound settings are saved locally on your device only. This data is never sent to us or to any third party. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No in-app purchases
  • No third-party services that receive your data

Children

Because we collect no data at all, the game does not collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
